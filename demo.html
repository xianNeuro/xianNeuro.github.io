Choose-your-own-adventure paradigm demo

To study agency in a naturalistic setting, we developed the choose-your-own-adventure (CYOA) paradigm. A story is presented one to three sentences at a time. At choice points, two options are shown as buttons; the story unfolds according to the option you pick.

How to read

  • Read the sentence in the box below.
  • Press Enter to go to the next sentence (anywhere on this page).
  • At a choice point, press Enter to reveal the two options, then click the one you want.
  • Keep reading until "The end."

A sense of agency. How would the story have unfolded had you chosen differently at a choice point? Press Restart to read it again from the beginning. Nothing you do here is recorded or sent anywhere.

returns to the first sentence

If nothing happens when you press Enter, click once inside the box and try again. Open the demo in its own tab. The demo loads Bootstrap, jQuery, and SortableJS from public content delivery networks, so it needs network access.
